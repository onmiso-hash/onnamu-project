Privacy Policy

What we record, why, and for how long

onnamu RDAP Service ("the Service") complies with the applicable laws of the Republic of Korea. This policy sets out what information the Service processes, what it is used for, and how long it is kept.

The Service has no sign-up process. It never asks you for a name, contact details or anything else that would identify you directly.

1. What is collected

The following is generated automatically for operational and security purposes.

a. Access records

  • Timestamp, IP address, country and city inferred from that address, requested path
  • HTTP response status, browser and operating system string (User-Agent)

b. Page-view signal

  • To count the pages people actually open, a randomly generated identifier is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key onnamu_vid. It is linked to no personal information whatsoever.
  • The signal carries only the time, the page path and the country. No IP address is recorded with it.
  • Clearing site data in your browser removes the identifier at any time.

c. What is not collected

  • Which domains or addresses you looked up is not stored against you as an individual.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or payment details are ever requested.

2. Purpose

  • Keeping the Service running and diagnosing faults
  • Detecting and blocking abusive or automated traffic
  • Producing aggregate usage statistics that do not identify individuals

3. Retention

Access records and page-view signals are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. No long-term archive is maintained, except where a law requires a specific retention period.

4. Cookies and third-party advertising

Advertising supplied by Google appears on the Service's editorial pages (About, Reading Results, FAQ and the policy pages). No advertising is placed on the lookup tool itself.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of third-party vendors' personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • See How Google uses data from sites that use its services for details.

You can refuse cookies in your browser settings; the lookup functions work regardless.

5. Disclosure and processors

Access records are never sold or handed to third parties. Given how the Service works, though, three things are worth flagging:

  • Query forwarding: RDAP lookups are redirected to the authoritative registry, so your browser connects to that server directly. Your IP address may reach it in the process, and what happens next is governed by that registry's own policy.
  • Network: traffic reaches the Service through Cloudflare's network, which is governed by Cloudflare's policies.
  • Advertising: as described in section 4, Google processes cookies for ad delivery.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a halt to the processing of your data; write to the address below and it will be handled without delay.

Note that domain registration data is neither held nor editable by this Service. The registrar and registry concerned maintain it, so corrections have to be requested from them.

7. Security measures

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Access records stay on the server, with restricted access, and are not exported.
  • On the operations dashboard, IP addresses are shown masked unless an administrator is signed in.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
